SK hynix Ships 12-High HBM4E Samples: What the Official Record Establishes

Aug 5, 2026Sail Research Lead source: SK hynix newsroom press release, 2026-06-18Fact / hypothesis separated

On 18 June 2026, SK hynix announced through its own newsroom that it had shipped samples of its 12-high HBM4E stack to major customers Direct. That is the load-bearing fact in this brief, and it moves the HBM4E transition from roadmap language to a dated, vendor-confirmed sample event — weeks after Samsung disclosed industry-first HBM4E sample shipments in May Attributed to Samsung. What the official record does not establish — customer identities, qualification passes, yield, or ramp dates — is where the decision-relevant uncertainty actually lives Requires verification.

What the official record establishes

SK hynix's 12-high HBM4E sample announcement is a first-party disclosure with concrete specifications: up to 16 Gbps per-pin bandwidth, power efficiency improved by more than 20% versus the previous generation, a 48 GB 12-high stack, Advanced MR-MUF packaging retained for structural stability and thermals, and thermal performance improved by roughly 17% against HBM4 Direct. The company also stated it will “work closely with partners for mass production in a timely manner” — an intent statement, not a dated commitment Attributed.

Samsung's earlier position: industry-first HBM4E sample shipments disclosed in May 2026 via Samsung's own channels and carried by trade press Attributed to Samsung. Micron's public HBM4 baseline remains its official IR disclosures of HBM4 36 GB 12-high high-volume production and 48 GB 16-high samples, as recorded in Sail Research's prior HBM tracking Direct, prior record.

An unresolved contradiction worth flagging

Two press narratives about NVIDIA's Vera Rubin HBM4 supplier set are circulating simultaneously: one attributes to NVIDIA's CEO a statement that all three memory vendors cleared certification (reported 2026-06-05) Attributed, press-carried; another reports one vendor excluded from initial builds, citing unnamed supply-chain sources Contradiction, unresolved. No NVIDIA official publication names an HBM4 memory supplier or qualification status. Until a primary NVIDIA document or each vendor's own qualification disclosure exists, Sail Research holds the supplier-set question open rather than picking a narrative Requires verification.

Our analysis (labeled): the HBM4E sample race is now evidenced at 12-high for both Korean vendors, while 16-high remains verification-dependent for all three; sample shipment is not qualification, and qualification is not ramp Inference.

Evidence Caveat — Fact vs. Hypothesis

  • Verified — SK hynix's newsroom announced 12-high HBM4E sample shipments to major customers on 2026-06-18, with the specifications as disclosed.
  • Attributed — Samsung's industry-first HBM4E sample claim and NVIDIA certification remarks are party statements carried by press, not independently established.
  • Inference — Sail analysis on readiness sequencing (12-high evidenced, 16-high open) is labeled analysis, not sourced fact.
  • Open Gap — Customer identities, qualification results, yield, ramp dates, and the Vera Rubin supplier set have no primary-source resolution.
  • What this report does not do — rank vendors by unverified allocation estimates, project volumes or pricing, or provide investment advice of any kind.
