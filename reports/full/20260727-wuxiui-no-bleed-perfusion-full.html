Wrong Platform, Right Patent: WO2020088180A1 Maps to WuXiUP, Not WuXiUI

Executive Summary

Our free sample (SR-2026-0008) framed patent WO2020088180A1 as “the patent behind WuXiUI.” Reading the patent’s verbatim claims against WuXi Biologics’ own platform descriptions shows that framing was wrong, and this report corrects it: the patent claims a continuous-harvest, no-bleed intensified perfusion architecture whose every independent-claim element matches the company’s WuXiUP™ continuous bioprocessing platform — while WuXiUI™ is, by the company’s own press releases, platform page, and a peer-reviewed methods paper, an ultra-intensified intermittent-perfusion fed-batch (UI-IPFB) that harvests in batch mode. The mapping presented here is our analysis, assembled from claim text and company self-descriptions; no company document states it, and we found no public source linking the patent to the WuXiUI brand. WuXiUI’s commercial claims (2,000 L GMP run at 18 g/L; 60–80% COGS reduction estimate) are real but company-announced; the platform’s strongest independently reviewed data point is a 24.5 g/L Mabcalin bispecific result in a peer-reviewed paper co-authored with the molecule’s owner, Pieris. Patent example performance figures (9.41×/6.56× productivity, >20 g/L accumulative Pv at 250 L) are evidence of WuXiUP-class capability and must not be cited as WuXiUI substantiation.

Mechanism & Teardown Analysis

What the patent legally covers. WO2020088180A1 has 50 claims and three independent claims (full verbatim text archived; this is technical analysis, not legal advice). Claim 1 (method) requires, in combination: (b) perfusing with a basal medium and a feed medium at different rates; the cell culture continuously passed through a separation system; and cells retained in the bioreactor without bleeding. Claim 42 (system) requires a perfusion module plus a module for continuously harvesting through a hollow-fiber filter whose cutoff passes the product and retains cells. Claim 41 is product-by-process. Dependent claims add ATF/TFF hardware (claims 2–3), hollow-fiber pore sizes of 0.08–0.5 µm (5–7), basal perfusion of 0.1 to ≤2.0 vessel volumes/day (8–11), feed at 0.1–20% of basal (12–15), a 35–37 °C → 28–33 °C temperature shift before peak cell density (16–19), accumulative volumetric productivity of ≥10/15/20 g/L (33–35), and continuous product capture over 2–16 chromatography columns (36–40). The bright-line elements are continuous passage, no bleed, and continuous harvest: a fed-batch process — even an ultra-intensified one that uses perfusion hardware episodically — does not practice claim 1.

Why that maps to WuXiUP. WuXi Biologics describes WuXiUP as “an intensified perfusion culture process” that “adopts continuous harvest to greatly reduce the product residence time,” and states it “couples continuous upstream process with continuous direct product capture using multicolumn chromatography system operating in a periodic countercurrent (PCC) mode.” That is claim 1’s continuous-harvest element and claims 36–40’s capture element in the company’s own words; the patent’s worked example even uses a three-column MabSelect PrismA capture setup. The company’s 105 g/L-over-20-days continuous run and 92% Protein A resin reduction (CEO keynote, company-stated) are reported in the accumulative, continuous-harvest framing the patent’s productivity claims (33–35) use.

Why it does not map to WuXiUI. Every company description of WuXiUI defines it as UI-IPFB: intermittent perfusion cycles around a fed-batch production culture, with the ATF device used for seed-train intensification and cell concentration, batch harvest (the 2,000 L run reports a single final “DS yield of 70%”), and an explicit selling point of “minimum changes to the downstream process” — the opposite of the patent’s continuous-capture coupling. The peer-reviewed WuXiUI methods paper (Zhang et al. 2024) describes UI-IPFB and never cites the patent. Notably, the company’s own WuXiUP page describes N-1 seed intensification feeding “regular fed-batch culture” as a separate, simpler option — the WuXiUI concept, framed by the company itself as the alternative to full continuous operation. A third-party academic paper on continuous capture refers to “intensified perfusion modes (WuXiUP process),” independently associating the continuous architecture with the WuXiUP name.

Legal status, stated precisely. The WO publication’s “Ceased” register entry means the PCT international phase has ended — it is not a loss of rights. National-phase family members are in force: CN111406105B (granted, Active), plus grants in Korea (KR102597919B1) and Japan (JP7707062B2); US and EP applications remain pending. The patent records five forward citations, including a competitor continuous-culture patent from AGC Biologics (WO2022081805A1).

A2 — Batch-economics model (WuXiUI 2,000 L GMP reference case)

Inputs. Working volume 2,000 L, titer 18 g/L, reported 4× multiple, DS yield 70% [C15]; typical culture 14 days [C11]. Assumptions: turnaround 5 days, range 3–7 [A1]; comparator fed-batch DS yield set equal at 70% [A2]; 365 operating days/year [A3]; implied comparator titer 18 ÷ 4 = 4.5 g/L (derivation D1 from the 4× claim [C15]; coincidentally matches the ~4.5 g/L pre-WuXiUI baseline in the canine case study [C16]).

Per batch. WuXiUI: 18 g/L × 2,000 L = 36 kg crude; ×70% = 25.2 kg DS/batch. Comparator: 4.5 g/L × 2,000 L = 9 kg; ×70% = 6.3 kg DS/batch. Ratio 25.2/6.3 = 4.0× — the model recovers the reported multiple (consistency check passed).

Per year (single train). Cycle = 14 d + turnaround. Base case [A1=5 d]: 365/19 = 19.2 batches × 25.2 kg ≈ 484 kg DS/year (range 438–541 kg for turnaround 7–3 d). Under linear scale-out [A4] to the marketed 6 × 2,000 L block [C12]: ≈2.9 t/year.

COGS consistency check. With per-batch facility/labor costs approximately equal [A5], a 4× mass gain cuts the per-kg facility term by ~75%; adding the disclosed DSP savings (50% time, 30–50% materials [C15]) makes the company’s 60–80% COGS-reduction estimate [C17] internally consistent, and the peer-reviewed 71% figure [C14] sits inside the band. This validates arithmetic, not the claim: no public cost model exists, and all performance inputs are company-origin.

Anti-conflation register. Four distinct figures that must never be merged: 18 g/L = WuXiUI 2,000 L GMP run [C15]; 24.5 g/L = Mabcalin bispecific, 16-day development run, peer-reviewed [C14]; 25 g/L = launch-PR example, unnamed bispecific, 14 days, PR-only [C13]; 105 g/L = WuXiUP accumulative output over 20 days at 50 L continuous — not a batch titer, not WuXiUI [C22]. Only the 18 g/L figure feeds the model, because it alone is tied to a GMP manufacturing scale.

What Would Break This Thesis

  • A company document explicitly linking WO2020088180A1 to WuXiUI (e.g., an investor deck or technical note naming the patent as WuXiUI’s basis). We searched and found none; if one surfaces, the correction thesis collapses to a branding-nuance story.
  • A WuXiUI methods disclosure showing continuous harvest or no-bleed operation in the production phase. Zhang et al. 2024 describes batch harvest for UI-IPFB; a contradictory primary disclosure would weaken element E2–E4 of the teardown.
  • Discovery of a separate WuXi patent family covering UI-IPFB specifically. That would not break the WO2020088180A1↔WuXiUP mapping, but it would break any implied claim that WuXiUI is unpatented.
  • Third-party contradiction of the WuXiUP self-description (e.g., evidence that WuXiUP is not continuous-harvest in practice), which would sever the claim-element match.
  • Register changes — lapse or invalidation of CN111406105B and the KR/JP grants would not break the architecture analysis but would void the enforcement-relevance framing.

Why not Evidence A

Grade A would require every load-bearing claim to rest on at least two independent primary sources, with a truthful independence statement. That bar is not met: (1) the 2,000 L GMP claim — the report’s most commercially load-bearing fact — is company-announced only, with all trade coverage derived from the same press release; (2) patent example performance is single-party disclosure by construction; (3) the WuXiUP performance column rests on company sources alone; and (4) the central thesis is our own anchored synthesis, which by definition cannot cite two independent sources for itself. What is independently supported: the patent record (two registers), the UI-IPFB definition of WuXiUI (company PR + peer-reviewed paper with Pieris co-authorship), and the industry benchmark context (independent peer-reviewed literature). Downgrading to B+ keeps those independence limits explicit rather than papering over them.

Evidence Caveat — Fact vs. Hypothesis

  • Fact (sourced) — the patent’s claim text and legal-status record; the company’s own platform definitions (WuXiUI = UI-IPFB fed-batch; WuXiUP = continuous harvest + PCC capture); the peer-reviewed 24.5 g/L Mabcalin result; the company-announced 2,000 L GMP run, attributed as company-announced.
  • Original analysis (ours, labeled) — the patent↔WuXiUP mapping, the WuXiUI/WuXiUP architecture comparison, and the batch-economics model. Presented with anchors and assumptions so they can be checked; not company statements.
  • Company-stated, independently unverified — 2,000 L run metrics, 25 g/L launch example, 60–80% COGS estimate, canine case study, all WuXiUP performance figures.
  • Requires verification — any customer- or regulator-side confirmation of WuXiUI/WuXiUP commercial performance; existence or non-existence of a separate UI-IPFB patent family.
  • What this report does not do — it does not give legal or freedom-to-operate advice; it does not treat patent examples as commercial-performance proof; it does not merge the 18/24.5/25/105 g/L figures; it does not claim the company endorses our platform–patent mapping.
