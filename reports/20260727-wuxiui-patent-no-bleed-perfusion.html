Superseded: No Bleed, Continuous Harvest — Original WuXiUI Patent Mapping

Published Jul 27, 2026Corrected Aug 2, 2026Sail Research

Original analysis preserved for audit

The material below is the original 27 July record and is not current research. Its repeated WuXiUI attribution is the error documented above. It remains visible only so readers can inspect what changed.

CDMO platform marketing is a fog of productivity multiples. Patents are the exception: they are the one public, primary document class in which a company must describe a technical method precisely enough to be examined. For WuXi Biologics' WuXiUI upstream intensification platform, that document is WO2020088180A1 — “Cell culture process by intensified perfusion with continuous harvest and without cell bleeding.” We read it, and this report separates what the patent text establishes from what it leaves unproven.

The Thesis

WuXiUI is not fed-batch optimization with a new name — the patent establishes a genuinely different upstream architecture (no-bleed intensified perfusion with continuous harvest), with example-level productivity above 20 g/L at 250 L. But the commercial-scale claims that circulate around the platform — 2,000 L GMP validation, 24.5 g/L on a bispecific, and end-to-end timeline compression — are not established by the public record we hold.

Why the Architecture Matters

Three design choices in the patent do the real work. Keeping cells in the reactor with no bleed preserves productive biomass that conventional perfusion discards. Differential basal/feed perfusion decouples nutrient supply from osmolality and metabolic-stress limits. And continuous harvest shortens product residence time in the bioreactor, which matters for less stable molecules and creates a natural coupling point for continuous downstream capture — the patent itself describes multi-column Protein A capture at the 15 L and 250 L example scales.

The honest read on timelines: a platform like this can plausibly compress upstream development by templating scale-up, but the patent does not prove it, and no public source we hold quantifies it. We carry that as inference, clearly labeled.

What This Report Cannot Tell You

Whether WuXiUI's economics beat Samsung Biologics' or Lonza's intensified offerings in commercial client programs; whether the 2,000 L GMP claim survives source retrieval; and any molecule-specific comparability outcome. Example-level patent data is real evidence of capability, but it is the assignee's own chosen examples — single-party by construction, which caps this report at Evidence B.

Evidence Caveat — Fact vs. Hypothesis

  • Fact (Direct Evidence, sourced) — the process architecture and example-level performance above, read directly from the published patent text.
  • Hypothesis / Reasoned Inference (not a fact) — CMC timeline compression and DSP-integration net benefit; plausible, unquantified.
  • Requires Verification — 2,000 L GMP validation and the 24.5 g/L Mabcalin bispecific figure, both carried in local research notes without retrievable primary sources.
  • What this report does not do — it does not treat patent examples as commercial-performance guarantees, and it does not compare economics against competing CDMO platforms.
