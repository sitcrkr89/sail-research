Research standards

How a claim earns the right to be called fact.

Sail Research is source-labeled evidence infrastructure—not a broker, newsroom, or investment adviser.

Every publication follows the same discipline: capture the primary source with a checkable record, grade each claim by how far it was verified, keep fact separate from interpretation, and never release without human review.

1

Hashed capture

Primary pages—issuer newsrooms, IR releases, patents, filings—are recorded with retrieval date, size, and SHA-256 hash alongside the URL. If the page changes later, the hash proves what it said the day we read it.

Example (SR-2026-0005): each vendor HBM4/HBM4E disclosure was captured with date + hash before claims were written.
2

Evidence grades

Internal labels roll up into the public grade on every report:

A
Primary-Verified
First-party sources verified by the operator.
B
Headline-Corroborated
Multi-outlet headline check; bodies not fully confirmed.
C
Hypothesis
Interpretation only—never stated as fact.
3

Fact / hypothesis boundary

Model scores and scenarios never masquerade as disclosed fact. Gaps stay labeled as gaps—not converted to zero or “evidence of absence.”

4

Operator review

A human reviews the trail before release. If support is thin, the grade comes down. Published work is observed for seven days; outcomes inform the next topic.

Preferred source classes

Issuer first-party disclosuresNewsrooms, IR, product pages—hashed for Grade A claims.
Patents & registriesFull-text reads with claim boundaries.
Official statistics & filingsCited to the original publisher with retrieval context.
Multi-outlet corroborationSupports Grade B only—not a substitute for primary verification.

Product boundaries

No quote product. No broker-report body hosting. No price targets or trading signals. Commissioned work uses the same grade rules as the public library.
