Research standards

How a claim earns the right to be called fact.

Sail Research is source-labeled evidence infrastructure—not a broker, newsroom, or investment adviser.

Every current publication follows one public model: preserve the source record, grade the support for the load-bearing conclusion, label each claim’s state, record the publication’s lifecycle status, and apply the release route defined in governance.

1

Hashed capture

Primary pages—issuer newsrooms, IR releases, patents, and filings—are recorded with retrieval date, size, URL, and a SHA-256 digest. The digest can show whether later bytes differ from the captured copy; it does not by itself prove authorship, truth, or what an unarchived page once said.

Example (SR-2026-0005): each vendor HBM4/HBM4E disclosure was captured with date + hash before claims were written.
2

Evidence grades

The publication grade assesses support for its load-bearing conclusion. It is not a score for prose quality, importance, or investment merit.

A
Independent primary chains
Every load-bearing conclusion is supported by at least two independent primary-source chains.
B+
Strong, bounded record
A multi-source primary record supports the central conclusion, but at least one material independence or verification limit remains explicit.
B
Attributable, limited
The conclusion is attributable to primary material, but relies on a single party, limited corroboration, or a material open verification gap.
C
Exploratory
The work is exploratory synthesis or hypothesis and must not be presented as established fact.
Archive entries keep the grade shown at original release and are marked “not regraded” when they predate Standards v2.0.
3

Claim state

Every material statement is one of four states: Verified means directly supported at the cited scope; Attributed means a named party says it; Inference is Sail analysis derived from disclosed evidence; and Open Gap means evidence is absent, inaccessible, conflicting, or still required. A report can contain all four states regardless of its overall grade.

4

Publication status and release control

Status is recorded separately as Active, Corrected, Archived, or Superseded. Routine briefs may pass a delegated deterministic preflight; Full Reports require explicit owner review under the current authorization. Automated gates are publication controls, not peer review.

A material correction preserves the original URL, marks it non-current, links both directions, and records the change in the public corrections ledger.

Preferred source classes

Issuer first-party disclosuresNewsrooms, IR, and product pages—valuable for attributable company claims, but not independent of the issuer.
Patents & registriesFull-text reads with claim boundaries.
Official statistics & filingsCited to the original publisher with retrieval context.
Independent primary recordsCustomer disclosures, regulator records, counterpart filings, and reproducible datasets can create a genuinely independent evidence chain.
Secondary reportingUseful for discovery and context; it does not become primary evidence through repetition.

Product boundaries

No quote product. No broker-report body hosting. No price targets or trading signals. Commissioned work uses the same grade rules as the public library.

Governance and corrections

The Editorial Governance page discloses release routes, automation, and independence rules. Material changes are recorded in the Corrections Ledger.
