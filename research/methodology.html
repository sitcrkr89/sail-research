Sources & methodology

How a claim earns the right to be called fact.

Sail Research is built as source-labeled evidence infrastructure. It is not a broker, trading terminal, or investment adviser.

Every public report runs the same discipline: capture the primary source with a checkable record, label each claim by how far it was verified, keep fact separate from interpretation, and never publish without human review of the trail.

1

Hashed capture

When a primary source is pulled—a company newsroom page, investor release, patent, or official filing—we record retrieval date, byte size, and a SHA-256 hash of the captured content alongside the URL. If the page changes later, the hash proves what it said the day we read it.

Example (SR-2026-0005): each vendor’s HBM4/HBM4E disclosure was captured with retrieval date + SHA-256 before claims were written.
2

Evidence grades

Internal labels (Direct Evidence · Analytic Prior · Requires Verification) roll up into the public grade on every report card:

A
Primary-Verified
Full-text / first-party sources verified by the operator.
B
Headline-Corroborated
Headline-level multi-outlet check; bodies not fully confirmed.
C
Hypothesis
Interpretation only—never stated as fact.
3

Fact / hypothesis boundary

Model scores, rankings, and scenarios are never presented as disclosed fact. Gaps are stated as gaps—not converted to zero or “evidence of absence.” Every mixed report carries an explicit boundary so readers see where verification ends.

Ruled out: treating a data gap as proof of absence; stating a model ranking as a disclosed fact; letting hedges stand in for sources.
4

Operator review

No report publishes on a pipeline’s say-so. A human reviews sources, hashes, labels, and the fact/hypothesis boundary before release. If the trail does not support the grade, the grade comes down. Published reports are tracked for seven days; outcomes feed the next topic selection.

Source classes we prefer

Issuer first-party disclosuresCompany newsrooms, IR releases, product pages—captured and hashed when used for Grade A claims.
Patents and official registriesFull-text reads with claim boundaries; marketing pages are secondary.
Official statistics and filingsWhen used, cited to the original publisher with retrieval context.
Multi-outlet headline corroborationSupports Grade B only—not a substitute for primary verification.

Missing values and corrections

Unavailable figures are omitted or labeled as open gaps—not filled with zeros. Material corrections are noted on the report; if the thesis or grade changes, the revision status says so.

How to cite Sail Research

Include report ID, publication date, evidence grade, and the primary sources listed on the card. Original disclosures remain authoritative; Sail states what those sources establish within a claim boundary.

Product boundaries

No exchange quote product. No broker-report body hosting. No price targets, recommendations, or trading signals. Commissioned work uses the same grade rules as the public library.
