Introduction

A large biopharmaceutical company developing two canine antibodies faced dual 
challenges of high cost of goods sold (COGS) and low titer. During upstream  
processing, the low titer was driving up production costs, bioreactor volumes, raw 
material use, and processing times—inefficiencies that reduced downstream yields 
and jeopardized commercial success.

Aiming to increase titer and decrease COGS, the company turned to WuXi Biologics 
for advanced process development. Maintaining the same commercial cell line was  
a key project requirement.

 
Challenges

Transferring a project to a contract research, development, and manufacturing  
organization (CRDMO) carries inherent risks, including quality concerns, higher 
costs, and extended timelines. As an experienced CRDMO with specialized animal 
health capabilities, WuXi Biologics quickly onboarded the canine antibody project 
and turned to focusing process refinements.

The original upstream process delivered a titer of only ~4.5 g/L, requiring yield  
improvements to support a more efficient downstream process. Downstream  
processing, however, also presented challenges:

• Small particles during harvest clogged filters, reducing yield and increasing  
 contamination risks.

• Weak Protein A affinity led to poor binding efficiency, greater resin use, and  
 limited scalability. Residual impurities posed quality risks.

• Low viral filtration capacity restricted purification, escalated material costs, 
 slowed processing, and created bottlenecks that compromised viral clearance.

Solution

WuXi Biologics strengthened upstream and downstream processes to enhance  
productivity, efficiency, and market competitiveness.

Upstream Productivity

The PawBio team synthesized cutting-edge technology with innovative process  
optimizations to deliver a comprehensive solution. WuXiUI™, WuXi Biologics’ 
technology platform for ultra-intensified fed-batch processing platform, brought a 
proven track record to upstream development:

• 3–6x productivity increases

• 60–80% lower COGS

• 2–4x higher cell concentrations in only 1.5–3 hours

• Upstream titers of 10–35 g/L for leading CHO cell lines

By surpassing traditional fed-batch processing, WuXiUI significantly reduced COGS 
and optimized biologic production. In this case study, the technology platform was 
instrumental in overcoming the upstream titer limitations that affected downstream 
processing and yield. 

Downstream Purification

To ensure high-quality purification during harvest, the PawBio team implemented  
a two-step purification process. This approach drove measurable gains:

• Eliminated small particles to prevent clogging and contamination

• Strengthened Protein A affinity for better binding efficiency and scalability.

• Increased viral filtration capacity, minimizing bottlenecks and lowering  
 material costs.

 
Results

The WuXiUI technology platform significantly enhanced upstream productivity, 
increasing the commercial cell line’s titer from ~4.5 g/L to 20.4 g/L - a 450%  
improvement. Downstream, the two-step purification process boosted overall yields 
to 65–70%, alleviating bottlenecks and improving efficiency.

The purification solution also provided process performance and product quality 
attributes comparable to pre-PawBio results, ensuring a consistent, scalable 
downstream solution. By maintaining the same commercial cell line, the process 
reinforced continuity and reliability, streamlining integration into future  
manufacturing efforts.
