•
Single-use tanks (SUTs) have a lower up-front capital investment, 
faster construction timelines & reduced indirect costs.

•
Based on data from 300+ large-scale batches, WuXi Bio believes 
that current manufacturing costs using SUTs remain favorable, 
especially when savings realized combining SUTs with advanced 
bioprocessing technology platforms

Manufacturing Efficiency 

& Flexibility

•
WuXi Bio has implemented scale-out strategies, achieving large-
scale production through parallel multiple SUB, with production 
scale up to 16,000L.

•
This scale-out approach eliminates the complexity and risk of 
traditional non-linear scale-up, allowing capacity to flex quickly 
with changes in demand.

•
Only 20% of commercial biologics require bioreactors > 10,000 L1.

Sustainability

•
Multiple studies have shown that SUT can significantly reduce 
the environmental footprint of biomanufacturing – using less 
energy, water and cleaning agents2 

•
For equivalent output, SUT can reduce water usage by up to 
70%, lower electricity consumption by ~30%, and cut CO2
emissions2 by ~25%.

•
These sustainability gains also help clients meet increasingly 
stringent ESG goals.
