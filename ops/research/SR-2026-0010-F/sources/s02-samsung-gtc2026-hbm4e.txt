"Industry-first commercial HBM4 and its successor HBM4E to be displayed, highlighting maximized performance, reliability and energy efficiency for next-generation datacenters"
"Samsung to present on strategic AI collaboration with NVIDIA in semiconductor engineering innovation spanning from R&D and design to manufacturing"

Samsung Electronics announced the comprehensive AI computing technologies it will showcase at NVIDIA GTC 2026 in San Jose, California, March 16-19 (booth #1207).

The centerpiece of Samsung's showcase at NVIDIA GTC 2026 is the new sixth-generation HBM4, which is now in mass production and is designed for the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform. Samsung's HBM4 delivers consistent processing speeds of 11.7 gigabits-per-second (Gbps), exceeding the industry standard of 8Gbps, and can be enhanced to 13Gbps.

Samsung HBM4E was displayed alongside HBM4 at the event.

--- NOTE ---
"Designed for the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform" is a vendor-side design-in claim; it is not an NVIDIA qualification confirmation.
