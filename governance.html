Institutional policy

Editorial governance

The controls behind what Sail publishes, how automation is used, and how material errors are corrected.

Scope. These rules apply to the public Sail Research library and commissioned research. They describe the current release process; they do not imply academic peer review, regulatory review, or legal assurance.

Two release routes

Routine public briefs may be prepared and released through a standing delegated workflow after deterministic checks for required metadata, source disclosure, evidence boundaries, and prohibited overclaim language. A passing automated gate is a publication control—not a guarantee of human review and not peer review.

Full Reports require explicit owner review before public release under the current internal authorization. The report itself states its sources, assumptions, verification gaps, and why it did not receive a higher evidence grade.

Automation and AI assistance

Research operations may use automated retrieval, parsing, comparison, drafting, and quality checks. Automation does not turn a source into independent evidence. Every public claim remains bounded by the cited record, and machine-generated synthesis must be labeled as analysis rather than source fact.

Evidence, claims, and status are separate

An evidence grade describes the support for a publication’s load-bearing conclusion. A claim state distinguishes verified fact, attributed statement, inference, and open gap. Publication status records whether an item is active, corrected, archived, or superseded. The definitions are maintained in the Research Standards.

Corrections and auditability

Material errors are not silently overwritten. The original URL remains available as a clearly marked, non-current audit record; the replacement links back to it; and the correction is entered in the public Corrections Ledger. Minor typographic or link repairs that do not change a conclusion may be updated without a ledger entry.

Commissioned-research independence

A client may define the decision question, scope, and delivery format. A client cannot buy a stronger grade, suppress a material contradiction, or convert an attributed statement into verified fact. Public release of commissioned work requires a separate publication decision.

Contact and confidential information

Forms on this static site create a draft in the visitor’s email application. The site does not receive or store the form contents before the visitor chooses to send that email. Do not include confidential, personal, export-controlled, or market-sensitive information in an initial inquiry.

Accountability contact

Send evidence challenges, correction requests, or governance questions to sail.research.info@gmail.com. Include the report ID, the exact disputed claim, and a primary-source link where possible.
