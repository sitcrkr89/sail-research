Before you treat a brief as decision-ready.

Sail Research is an independent evidence desk for Korea deep-tech bottlenecks—not a broker, not a news feed, not an investment adviser.

Global teams often face a gap: Korean and first-party technical disclosures matter, but headlines, sell-side narratives, and generic AI summaries collapse under challenge. Sail closes that gap with one rule—state only what sources establish, and label everything else.

What this desk is

A coverage-accountable desk for semiconductors (HBM, packaging, foundry), biopharma (CDO/CRO/CDMO, ADC), and power infrastructure. Public briefs demonstrate the standard. Paid work applies it to your decision question.

What this desk is not

Not a stock screener, quote terminal, or broker-report host. Not a price-target service. Not a substitute for legal, compliance, or investment advice.

Independence

Public reports are not paid placements. Commissioned clients do not purchase a higher Evidence Grade. Where a material relationship could affect perception of independence, we decline or disclose before work begins.
