About the institute

Independent. Claim-bounded. Human-reviewed.

Sail Research is an independent institute producing decision-grade evidence on Korean industry bottlenecks—not a broker, newsroom, or investment adviser.

Global teams often face a gap: Korean and first-party technical disclosures matter, but headlines and generic summaries collapse under challenge. Sail closes that gap with one rule—state only what sources establish, and label everything else.

What we are

A coverage-accountable research desk for semiconductors, biopharma, and power infrastructure. Public briefs demonstrate the standard. Commissioned work applies it to your decision question.

What we are not

Not a stock screener or quote terminal. Not a price-target service. Not a substitute for legal, compliance, or investment advice.

Independence

Public reports are not paid placements. Commissioned clients do not purchase a higher Evidence Grade. Material relationships are declined or disclosed before work begins.
