About the desk

An independent evidence desk for Korea bottlenecks.

Sail Research publishes and commissions decision-grade intelligence where local primary sources change the global decision context—semiconductors, biopharma, and power infrastructure.

Global teams often face a gap: Korean and first-party technical disclosures matter, but headlines, sell-side narratives, and generic AI summaries collapse under challenge. Sail exists to close that gap with a simple rule—state only what sources establish, and label everything else.

We are not a newsroom, not a brokerage, and not a volume content farm. Public briefs demonstrate the standard. Paid work applies the same standard to your decision question.

What “desk” means

A desk is accountable for coverage quality, not for feed velocity. Core topics receive capture, grading, and human review. Adjacent experiments may appear in the library archive without pretending to be Core coverage.

Independence

Public reports are not paid placements. Commissioned clients do not purchase a higher Evidence Grade. Where a material relationship could affect perception of independence, we decline or disclose before work begins.
